title: Build Log 2024-25
---

Running log of major subsystem decisions for the 2024–25 competition robot.

### Drivetrain

Settled on a tank drive over mecanum for this season — simpler build, more reliable under load, and easier for new drivers to learn quickly.

### Scoring Mechanism

Iterated through three intake designs before landing on the current linkage-driven claw. Prioritized cycle time over maximum reach.

### Autonomous

Autonomous routines run on the REV Control Hub using odometry-based positioning, tuned against our practice field markings.
