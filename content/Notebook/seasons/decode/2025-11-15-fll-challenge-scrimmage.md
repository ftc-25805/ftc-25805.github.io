# Hosting an FLL Challenge Scrimmage: Helping Rookie Teams Get Ready

*Posted November 15, 2025 · Team 25805 Reprogrammed*

On November 15, Reprogrammed hosted a scrimmage for the FIRST LEGO League Challenge program. The goal was simple: give newer teams a low-pressure chance to practice running the robot game before their real competitions.

## Practice Before the Real Thing

A scrimmage is a practice event. Teams get to run their robots on the table, see how a competition day works, and find out what needs fixing while there's still time to fix it. For a rookie team, that experience is a big help.

Our school's **three Dayton Christian School FLL teams** took part, along with **five other rookie teams**, for eight teams in all.

## How Our FTC Team Helped

Members of our FTC team helped run the day. We:

- **Refereed matches**, making sure the teams got a feel for how scoring and rules work at a real event.
- **Ran the scrimmage**, helping keep the schedule moving and the day organized.

Many of us have been on the other side of the table, so it was fun to help younger teams through the experience.

## Fundraising for Reprogrammed

We also held a fundraiser during the scrimmage to support our FTC season. We sold:

- Pizza
- 3D prints
- Other items

Thank you to everyone who bought something. Your support helps pay for registration, parts, and travel for our competition season.

## Thank You

Thank you to all eight teams for coming, to the coaches and families who supported them, and to everyone who helped us run the day. Good luck to all of the teams at their upcoming competitions!
