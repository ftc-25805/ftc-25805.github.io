# DC Robotics FLL Explorer Festival: Celebrating Our Youngest Builders

*Posted January 10, 2026 · Team 25805 Reprogrammed*

Not every robotics event is about winning matches. On January 10, Reprogrammed hosted the **DC Robotics FLL Explorer Festival** at Dayton Christian School, a celebration of the hard work our school's FIRST LEGO League Explorer teams have put in this year.

## A Festival for Our Explorer Teams

FIRST LEGO League Explorer is where many young students first discover robotics, and these teams spent the season learning, building, and getting creative. The festival gave them a chance to show off what they've made to their families, classmates, and our school community.

Five Explorer teams took part, each made up of six to eight students. That meant a gym full of young builders proudly explaining their projects to anyone who stopped by.

## Our Team Helped Judge

Members of our FTC team joined school employees to serve as judges. It was a great chance for us to give back. Not long ago, many of us were in the same spot, standing next to a LEGO model and explaining it to a curious adult. Talking with the Explorer teams about what they built and learned was a reminder of why we love this program.

## Fundraising for Reprogrammed

Alongside the festival, we ran two fundraisers to support our FTC season:

- **Donuts:** a table that was popular with hungry families.
- **Dayton Christian School Robotics gear:** shirts and merchandise so that supporters can show their team pride.

Every purchase helps cover registration fees, parts, and travel for our competition season. Thank you to everyone who bought a donut or picked up some gear!

## Thank You

Congratulations to all five Explorer teams on a great year. Thank you to the school staff who helped judge and organize, to the families who came to cheer, and to everyone who supported our fundraisers. We can't wait to see these students on the FTC field someday.
