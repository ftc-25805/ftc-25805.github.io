# Lebanon Qualifier Recap: We're Headed to the Ohio Championship

*Posted January 17, 2026 · Team 25805 Reprogrammed*

Our second qualifier of the DECODE season is done. This weekend, Reprogrammed traveled to the Warren County Fairgrounds Event Center in Lebanon for the [2025 Lebanon Qualifier](https://ftc-events.firstinspires.org/2025/USOHLEQ). Twenty-three teams competed, and four spots at the Ohio Championship were up for grabs. We're thrilled to share that **Reprogrammed qualified for the Ohio Championship!**

## Qualification Matches

We finished qualifications ranked **7th of 23 with a 3-2-0 record**, averaging 2.60 ranking points. That matches the record we put up in Dayton, and we were one win away from sharing the 4-1 group near the top.

## Think Award, 2nd Place

The highlight of the weekend came off the field. Reprogrammed took home the **Think Award, 2nd Place**. The Think Award recognizes the team that best shows the engineering process: how we design, test, and improve the robot. It's a great reflection of the work this team puts into its notebook, its design decisions, and its code.

## How the Event Finished

The Walnut Hills "NUTS!" (6133) captained the winning alliance with Higgs Bosons (7136) as their first pick. Nuts & Bolts (5040) captained the finalist alliance alongside Robo Kai (17978).

Congratulations to the other award winners:

- **Inspire Award:** 5040 Nuts & Bolts (2nd: 6133 The "NUTS!", 3rd: 16449 Juniper Robotics)
- **Design Award:** 7136 Higgs Bosons
- **Control Award:** 22448 R.A.T.S.
- **Innovate Award:** 9952 CHCA Beak Squad
- **Think Award:** 10464 The Bionic Tigers
- **Connect Award:** 17978 Robo Kai
- **Sustain Award:** 14365 Summit Knights
- **Reach Award:** 5140 WACO Aerobotics
- **Judges' Choice Award:** 30769 Cyber Kings

## What's Next: The Ohio Championship

Qualifying for the Ohio Championship is a big step for the team. We'll review our match footage, work through what held us back from a better record, and keep improving the robot and our code to get ready for the championship. Thank you to the volunteers, judges, and referees, to the Warren County Fairgrounds for hosting, and to every team we played with and against.
