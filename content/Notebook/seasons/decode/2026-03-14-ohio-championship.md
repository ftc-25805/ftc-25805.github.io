# Ohio Championship Recap: Our Season at Hobart Arena

*Posted March 14, 2026 · Team 25805 Reprogrammed*

This weekend, Reprogrammed competed at the [2025 Ohio Championship](https://ftc-events.firstinspires.org/2025/USOHCMP) at Hobart Arena in Troy. Thirty-eight of the best teams in the state came together for three days of competition, and our team earned its place on that field with a qualifying finish at the Lebanon Qualifier in January.

## Qualification Matches

This one didn't go our way on the field. We finished qualifications ranked **31st of 38 with a 1-3-1 record** and 1.20 ranking points. The competition at the top was very strong, and several teams went undefeated through qualifications:

## How the Event Finished

Gyrobotic Droids (23435) captained the winning alliance with Team Calamari (10084) as their first pick. Innovators Robotics (3311) captained the finalist alliance alongside Fe2O3 (21865).

Congratulations to the award winners:

- **Inspire Award:** 20744 24 Karat (2nd: 6133 The "NUTS!", 3rd: 10464 The Bionic Tigers)
- **Think Award:** 16449 Juniper Robotics (2nd: 17978 Robo Kai)
- **Connect Award:** 8120 Electric Hornets (2nd: 23927 INTERSTELLAR)
- **Sustain Award:** 23493 Techno Domination (2nd: 12802 We Will Bot You)
- **Innovate Award:** 5140 WACO Aerobotics (2nd: 32896 Droid Squad)
- **Control Award:** 23435 Gyrobotic Droids (2nd: 3311 Innovators Robotics)
- **Reach Award:** 21865 Fe2O3 (2nd: 16657 We Did Start the Fire)
- **Design Award:** 10084 Team Calamari (2nd: 12569 STEMstangs)
- **Judges' Choice Award:** 30334 Blood, Sweat, and Gears

## Looking Back on the Season

Getting to the state championship in our DECODE season took a lot of work from everyone on the team. We started in Dayton with a 7th-place qualification finish, followed it with another 7th-place finish and a Think Award 2nd Place in Lebanon, and earned our way to Troy. Not every event goes the way you hope, but we're taking what we learned from the best teams in Ohio back to the workshop.

Thank you to the volunteers, judges, and referees who made the championship possible, to Hobart Arena for hosting, and to our families, mentors, and sponsors for supporting us all season. We'll see you next season!
