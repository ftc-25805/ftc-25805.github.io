# Dayton Qualifier Recap: Our First Event of the DECODE Season

*Posted December 13, 2025 · Team 25805 Reprogrammed*

The first qualifier of the DECODE season is in the books. This weekend, Reprogrammed made the trip to Building 8 at Sinclair Community College in Dayton for the [2025 Dayton Qualifier](https://ftc-events.firstinspires.org/2025/USOHDAQ). Four spots at the Ohio Championship were on the line, and the field showed up ready to compete.

## Qualification Matches

We finished qualifications ranked **7th out of the field with a 3-2-0 record**, averaging 2.80 ranking points. Just behind the top six, that's a solid result for our first event with this game.

## How the Event Finished

Droid Squad (32896) captained the winning alliance with Gyrobotic Droids (23435) as their first pick, while Innovators Robotics (3311) captained the finalist alliance alongside OZone Yellow (31642).

Congratulations to the award winners:

- **Inspire Award:** 23435 Gyrobotic Droids (2nd: 3311 Innovators Robotics, 3rd: 11781 MVS Rambotics)
- **Design Award:** 32896 Droid Squad
- **Control Award:** 27125 OZone Purple
- **Innovate Award:** 31640 OZone Red
- **Think Award:** 5040 Nuts & Bolts
- **Connect Award:** 7136 Higgs Bosons
- **Sustain Award:** 27848 Bot Ballers
- **Reach Award:** 25632 Mean Green Irish Machine
- **Judges' Choice Award:** 31642 OZone Yellow

## What's Next

Every event teaches us something. We'll be going back through our match footage, finding what worked and what didn't, and putting it into the robot and our code before our next competition.

Thank you to the volunteers, judges, and referees who ran the event, to Sinclair Community College for hosting, and to every team we shared the field with. See you at the next one!
