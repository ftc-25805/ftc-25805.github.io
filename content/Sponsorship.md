Thank you for your interest in supporting our robotics team! We are always looking for partners who share our passion for STEM education and innovation. See our [[Build Log 2024-25|build log]] for how your support shows up on the robot.

## Why Sponsor Us?

- **Community Impact** — help inspire the next generation of engineers
- **Visibility** — brand exposure with students, families, and the robotics community
- **Partnership** — work directly with students on engineering challenges
- **Educational Value** — support hands-on STEM learning
